## Contract and behavior

An optional list of 1–16 schedules shares one task. Its first entry is mirrored into legacy
`schedule`; conflicting mirrors are refused. Legacy stored single schedules still load. A
schedule-only old-client PUT replaces the entire list, and the CLI preserves untouched lists
while making explicit timing updates replacement. Once cannot be combined with another trigger.
One union due instant and the existing `schedule:<instant>` receipt deduplicate coincident
times. Catch-up chooses only the newest missed union occurrence, scanning at most nine days.
Any timing edit clears the due state under the mutation lease; stale timers cannot restore it.
