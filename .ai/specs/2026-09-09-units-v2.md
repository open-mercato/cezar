## TLDR

The unit hierarchy (Caesar → Legate → Centurion, spec `2026-09-08-units-hierarchy.md`) could not
reliably delegate: a by-the-book spawn was refused by the marker parser, a refusal was fatal, a
settled child's budget was never released, a restart reported an unanswered question as `done`,
and every rank below the root knew only its own order. This spec records what changed:

1. **The engine can delegate** — parsers anchor on the LAST marker occurrence and tolerate a
   trailing `CEZ:MONITORING`/`CEZ:DONE` line; a refused marker is delivered back into the
   session as a re-prompt; the autonomous nudge is reachable on a unit's first turn and never
   fires past a spawn, a budget halt or a unit's own question; a settled child's unspent budget
   returns to its parent; the pending question is persisted and a restart reports it `blocked`.
2. **The mission directory** — `.ai/cezar/missions/<missionId>/` is the filesystem channel between
   units: the brief, the plan, every unit's order/notes/report, and an inbox per unit. Files are
   the content; the engine is the signal.
3. **Flexible composition** — a spawn names a `rank` (any rank below the spawner) and a `kind`
   (`implement`, `review`, `research`, `plan`); reviewers judge another unit's branch and answer
   with a `verdict`. The Legate is the management layer.
4. **Per-mission resources** — `parallel` (a mission-scoped concurrency limit, exempt from the
   workspace cap) and `maxChildren` (children in flight per commander), set at mission start.
5. **Escalation one rank at a time** — questions for a commander go through its inbox and a
   monitoring park; `CEZ:ASK` is reserved for the human; a blocked child is announced to its
   commander.

## Resolved assumptions

| # | Question | Applied default | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Files vs markers for inter-unit messages | Files. No new marker for messaging or escalation. | The user's decision; a file is readable by every rank and every tool, survives restarts, and needs no schema. |
| A2 | Who fires a delivery | The WRITER's turn end (`notifyMissionInboxes`), the RECIPIENT's own turn end (`deliverOwnInbox`), and the recipient's session open (`flushInbox`). No watcher, no timer. | AGENTS.md "who fires this?": every transition is on an edge cezar already owns. |
| A3 | Which parked runs are woken live | Only a run parked as a MONITOR. A run parked `waiting` (Guard, budget) gets the digest at its next session open. | A message must never stand in for the human's answer to a Guard question. |
| A4 | Concurrency | A mission-scoped `parallel`; the mission's runs are exempt from the workspace/project caps and admitted under the mission's count. Absent = today. | Widening the global knob widens every project; a mission that wants to run wide says so at start. |
| A5 | Verification | A REVIEW unit (kind) spawned by the commander, of a different unit than the author; the commander merges only after `approve`. | User decision (review centurion over engine-run commands). |
| A6 | The Legate | Stays, as the management layer. A caesar may still spawn centurions directly with `rank`. | User decision. |
| A7 | Unanswered question across a restart | Settle as before, report `blocked` naming the question; the `pendingAsk` stays on the terminal record. | The parent trusts the report, not cezar's status; the trace of what went unanswered must survive. |

## The mission directory

```
.ai/cezar/missions/<missionId>/
  brief.md                  objective, constraints, resources — written by POST /missions
  plan.md                   Caesar's order of battle (Caesar maintains it)
  ledger.jsonl              engine events: mission-start, spawn, settle, inbox-notice, guard-ask
  units/<id8>/order.md      the task order (engine, at spawn)
  units/<id8>/notes.md      the unit's running notes (seeded once; "Suggestions for the mission")
  units/<id8>/report.md     the settle report (engine, at settle)
  inbox/<id8>/*.md          messages to a unit — from any unit or the engine
  inbox/root/*.md           messages to the mission root
```

- Every unit run gets `CEZ_MISSION_DIR` and the directory in its allowed directories; its task
  order names its brief, order, notes and inbox (`missionEnvelopeLines`).
- `unit.inboxSeenAt` is the per-run watermark; `listInbox` returns files newer than it.
- Suggestions: `report.suggestions[]` (≤8) plus the notes' section are copied to `inbox/root/` at
  settle. Suggest, never redefine — the objective is the user's.
- A child parking on `CEZ:ASK` writes `Blocked on a Guard question — …` into its commander's inbox.
- Module: `packages/cezar/src/units/mission-fs.ts` (pure, sync, best-effort writes).

## Contract deltas (`packages/contract/src/units.ts`)

- `unitKindSchema` = `implement | review | research | plan`; `unitResourcesSchema` = `{ parallel?, maxChildren? }`.
- `unitSchema` + `kind`, `reviewOf[]`, `resources` (root only), `inboxSeenAt`, `pendingAsk { requestId?, questions[], askedAt }`.
- `unitSpawnSchema` children + `rank`, `kind`, `review_of[]`; the per-payload bound is 32 (sanity), the real cap is the mission's `maxChildren`.
- `unitReportSchema` + `verdict`, `suggestions[]`.
- `startMissionInputSchema` + `parallel`, `maxChildren`.

## Engine (`packages/cezar/src/workflows/run.ts`)

- `tryAutonomousNudge(runId, state, stepId, ask, unitTurn)` — one helper, both turn-end sites, with the three unit exceptions.
- `handleUnitMarkers` → `rePromptRefusals`, `deliverOwnInbox`, `notifyMissionInboxes`; `recordAsk` persists `pendingAsk` and notifies the commander; `deliverMessage` clears it.
- `spawnChildren` resolves `childRoleFor(spawner, rank)`, composes the kind addendum, enforces `resources.maxChildren`, writes order/notes/ledger.
- `busySlots` exempts mission-limited runs; `pump` admits them under `missionInFlight < parallel`.
- `remainingBudgetUsd` charges a settled child its actual cost.

## Prompts (`packages/cezar/src/units/prompts.ts`)

`MISSION_FS_RULE` (brief first, notes, inbox, escalation one rank at a time) in every role; the Guard
names the in-worktree child merge as the one merge it does not cover; the edit ban is
integration-only; the spawn line is the last thing in a turn; `UNIT_KIND_PROMPTS` addenda;
the Legate as management layer; the report contract's `suggestions`.
