CezarBot · a clearer “Devices” screen

Phase 1 design for the connect page's list screen (packages/mobile/connect/, shared by iOS and Android). Every mockup below is live: tap a cezar to open it, tap ⋯ for its settings, try Forget. The Name control swaps the noun everywhere, since the list can hold a laptop, a VPS or a cloud host. Spec: .ai/specs/2026-11-19-mobile-devices-screen.md.

How you get here, and back

Manager→ Devices link / banner → Switcher sheet (native)→ “Manage cezars” → Your cezars
  • The top-left ← carries the name of the cezar you came from (“← MacBook Pro”), and returns to its Manager, as cameFrom does today. Android Back and the iOS edge swipe do the same.
  • Tapping any card opens that cezar at /manager (Decision 3 of the manager-first spec); tapping the current one just goes back.
  • On a cold start with no cezars, this screen is the empty state: pairing comes first.
  • The native switcher keeps its job (quick switching over a page) but renames its two actions to Connect another cezar and Manage cezars; the banner's button becomes Cezars.

Accessibility

  • Every target is at least 44×44 pt (iOS) / 48 dp (Android): cards are 76 tall, the ⋯ button 44.
  • A card is one button whose label reads like the native switcher's: “MacBook Pro, online, 2 need you, current. Opens its Manager.” The ⋯ button is separate: “Settings for MacBook Pro”.
  • Status is never colour alone: a word always sits beside the dot.
  • Switches use role="switch" with aria-checked; section titles are headings so VoiceOver's rotor and TalkBack's heading navigation jump between the list and “App”.
  • Forget's sheet traps focus, starts on Cancel, and Escape / Back closes it.
  • Respects Dynamic Type / font scale (no fixed heights on text), reduced motion, light and dark.
