Jump between your own messages

A small segmented pill — older ▲ · 💬 your messages · ▼ newer, with no numbers — that grows out of the existing “Jump to latest” button whenever you scroll back through history, on every screen size, in both the task thread and the manager chat. At the bottom of the chat there is nothing. The arrows step through the messages you sent; the middle button opens the list of all of them. On phones the list is a bottom sheet.

Recommendation

One control, everywhere (revision 4). The “Jump to latest” button grows into one combined pill when you scroll up: ▲ · 💬 · ▼ | ↓ Latest on desktop and ▲ · 💬 · ▼ | ↓ on phones. It leaves again when you are back at the bottom. It sits exactly where “Jump to latest” floats today, so nothing new is spread across the screen and no space is used at rest. The manager's round ↓ button becomes the same pill, so both views match.

No numbers, revision 3. The pill shows no counter: in a long chat the loaded range may not hold every message, and with several of your messages on one screen no single number is right. The middle 💬 segment just opens the list: a popover on desktop (hover or click) and a bottom sheet on touch screens. With no message from you, nothing is rendered.

Desktop · task transcript

The mockup opens at the bottom, so there is no pill. Scroll up and “Jump to latest” grows into the pill. Then ▲ / ▼ scroll the transcript and highlight the message they land on. The control, state by state

These are the real component styles, frozen in each state.

Where it sits: options considered

A · In the chat header

  • + Never overlaps content, always in the same place.
  • − Far from where your eyes and pointer are. You read at the bottom, then travel to the top to step, back and forth.
  • − The task header on a phone has no room left (title, ⋯, expand, tabs), and it folds away on scroll.

B · Grows out of “Jump to latest” Recommended

  • + One control for moving through the transcript, in the one place that already does it. Nothing new is spread across the screen.
  • + Takes no space at rest. It appears exactly when you are reading back, which is when you need it.
  • + The same on desktop, tablet, phone and the app shell, and the same in both views.
  • − At the bottom you scroll up once, or press ⌥↑, to reach it.

C · On the dock's status line (revision 2)

  • + Right above the textarea, and it never covers the transcript.
  • − Always visible, and a second control away from “Jump to latest”, so the transcript controls are split across two places.
  • − On phones it costs a permanent line when no hint is showing.

D · Ticks beside the scrollbar

  • + Shows how your messages are spread across the transcript at a glance.
  • − The positions are unknown for ranges that aren't loaded, and virtua's sizes are estimates.
  • − Too small to tap, and iOS hides the scrollbar entirely. Fine as a later extra, not as the control.

Behavior notes

What ▲ / ▼ do

  • ▲ goes to the nearest message of yours whose top is above the top of the viewport; ▼ goes to the nearest one below it. A jump aligns the message to the top with 24 px of breathing room, so pressing it repeatedly walks back steadily.
  • ▲ is disabled only when no message of yours exists above the viewport in any range, loaded or not. ▼ is disabled when every message below is already fully on screen.
  • No counter. In a long chat the loaded range may not hold every message, and when several of yours share the screen no single index is right. Where you are shows in the list instead: every message of yours that is on screen gets the violet bar.

The highlight

This is the #2218 pattern: scrollIntoView, then focus({preventScroll}) on the bubble (tabIndex=-1), drawn with the cockpit's ring-[3px] ring-ring/50. A short violet wash (1.8 s) helps the eye find it after a long scroll. The ring stays until focus moves on.

With reduced motion: no smooth scroll (behavior:'auto') and no wash. The ring alone marks the message. Tick the checkbox above to try it.

Ranges that aren't loaded

  • Task: useRunHistory keeps at most 5 pages, so the list can't be built from the loaded events. A small index route, GET …/runs/:id/user-messages (seq, ts, preview ≤ 240 chars, attachment kinds), feeds the list and the counter. A jump to an unloaded seq pages back with the same refill as #1909 until that seq is loaded, then reveals it.
  • Manager: ?kind=user on the messages route gives the list. A jump runs the #2218 loop: fetchNextPage until the id is loaded, then reveal.
  • While it loads, the list row shows a spinner and the transcript shows its normal “Loading earlier messages…” boundary. A failure shows a toast and leaves you where you were.

The list

  • Oldest at the top and newest at the bottom, like the transcript. The list opens upward from a pill at the bottom, so the newest messages sit right next to the pointer. It opens scrolled to the current message.
  • Day headers (“Today”, “Yesterday”, “Nov 14”) are sticky, and the time column stays HH:mm. A long chat scrolls inside a list capped at 328 px (popover) or 72 % of the screen (sheet).
  • The message in view gets a violet bar and aria-current="true". The keyboard-active row gets a ring.

Truncation and attachments

  • The preview is the message as plain text: markdown stripped, newlines folded to “ · ”, whitespace collapsed, clamped to two lines with line-clamp. The full text is the row's title on desktop.
  • A diff-review message (comments drafted on Changes) previews as “Review · 3 comments” followed by its text.
  • Attachments: an image icon or a paperclip with a count, placed right of the row. A message with only an attachment previews as “Image” or “poller-notes.md”, in muted text.

While new messages stream in

  • A message you send is added to the list at once, as “Sending…”. A task's queued messages (queuedMessages) appear with a “Queued” label. Neither one moves the keyboard-active row or steals focus.
  • The count goes up live. If you are pinned to the tail, the stick-to-bottom behavior is unchanged. A jump un-pins you, exactly as a scroll up does, and “Jump to latest” appears.
  • Agent output streaming in changes nothing in the pill. If the list is open, the on-screen bars follow the scroll.

Accessibility

  • A role="group" labelled “Your messages”. The buttons are “Previous message of yours”, “Next message of yours” and “Your messages” (aria-haspopup="listbox", aria-expanded).
  • The list is a listbox with aria-activedescendant: ↑↓ HomeEnd move, Enter jumps, Esc closes and returns focus to the button. A polite live region announces “Jumped to your message from 9:41”.
  • Shortcuts: ⌥↑ / ⌥↓ while focus is in the transcript (never in the composer), registered in keybindings.ts so they can be rebound.

Hover vs click, desktop vs touch

  • With a pointer that can hover, hovering for 150 ms opens a peek, which closes 250 ms after the pointer leaves. A click pins it open until Esc, an outside click or a jump. Keyboard focus never opens it, but Enter does.
  • On no-hover: devices, a tap opens the bottom sheet. Arrows react to the tap itself, never to a long press.
  • The pill shows and hides with the existing away-from-tail signal (pillVisible / scroll.away), so it adds no new scroll listener. Returning to the bottom closes an open list.
  • With no message of yours in the chat, it falls back to today's plain “Jump to latest” button.
  • At the bottom, ⌥↑ still jumps to your last message, which brings the pill in.
