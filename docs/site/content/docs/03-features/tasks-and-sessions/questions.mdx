The card shows the question and available answers when an agent is waiting for a decision.

## Where to find it

Task thread → question card

## How to use it

1. Open the waiting task and read the question and its available options.
2. Send your answer.

Every open question from your tasks and managers is also listed on the **Questions** page, where you can answer or dismiss it without opening the task. A question closes when you answer it from its card, when the agent asks it again or withdraws it, or when the task ends; a reply typed in the chat leaves it open, marked **May be out of date**.

> **Good to know**: Question cards are available when the selected agent and execution mode support questions.
