The microphone in the message field starts speech recognition. When you finish, you can insert the transcript into a draft or send it immediately. You can also cancel the recording.

## Where to find it

Message field → microphone

## How to use it

1. Click Start dictation and allow microphone access.
2. Dictate your instruction.
3. Choose Insert transcription to review it, or Insert transcription and send.

> **Good to know**: Dictation requires a supported browser and HTTPS or localhost. Older desktop app versions may need an update.
