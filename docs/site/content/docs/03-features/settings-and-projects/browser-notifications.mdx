Your browser can notify you about tasks while the Cezar tab is in the background.

## Where to find it

Settings → Notifications

## How to use it

1. Enable Notify when an agent needs you.
2. Allow notifications in your browser.
3. Keep the Cezar tab open in the background.

> **Good to know**: Notifications are off by default. Your browser must support them and have your permission to display them. This feature does not send push notifications from a native phone app.
