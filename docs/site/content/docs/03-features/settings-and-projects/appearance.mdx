The available options are Light, Dark, and System.

## Where to find it

Settings → Appearance → Theme

You can open **Global Settings** from the sidebar’s **More** menu (the three dots beside the version). It opens Appearance and stays selected throughout global Settings. On a phone, open the navigation menu first. Theme choices are also available directly in **More**.

## How to use it

1. Choose Light, Dark, or System.
2. Select System if the theme should follow your system settings.

> **Good to know**: Your selection is saved for this browser.
